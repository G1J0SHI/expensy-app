# Privacy Policy

Our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by the Application and how we use it.

## Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed as a completely offline personal finance companion. It does not require account creation, sign-ups, or subscriptions. All data-including transactions, categories, budgets, accounts, and liquid net worth calculations-is stored strictly on your local device using local storage. The Application does not collect, record, or transmit any personal identifiable information, device identifiers, or analytics.

## Device Permissions

The Application does not request any sensitive permissions or internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET`). All features, local data persistence, and calculations operate entirely offline on your device without network communication.

## Data Backup and Ownership

The Application offers an optional one-tap JSON backup and restore feature via your system clipboard. Exported data is handled directly by you and copied only to your device's clipboard; no data is ever transmitted to or stored on any external server or cloud service.

## Security

I value your trust in using our Application. Because no personal or financial information is collected or transmitted over the internet, your information remains entirely within your control on your hardware.

## Links to Other Sites

This Application does not contain third-party advertisements or external tracking links. If you visit any external developer or store links, you are advised to review the Privacy Policy of those respective sites.

## Children’s Privacy

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, monitor and guide their online activity.

The Application does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
