Expensy is a clean, minimal personal finance companion built to help you track every penny, plan monthly budgets, and master your money with zero friction.

Designed with functional simplicity: no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions, and no cloud trackers-just lightning-fast tracking, proactive budget alerts, and clear financial clarity right on your device.

---

### ✨ CORE FEATURES IN ONE POWERFUL EXPENSE TRACKER

- **⚡ Instant Transaction Logging** - Record expenses, income, and account-to-account transfers in seconds with quick-amount chips (+100, +500, +1000), categories, and notes.
- **🎯 Smart Category Budgets** - Set monthly spending limits per category with visual progress bars and proactive alert threshold warnings before you overspend.
- **🏦 Multi-Account & Net Worth** - Track bank accounts, cash in hand, digital wallets, and savings in one unified view with live liquid net worth calculation.
- **📊 Visual Analytics & Insights** - Interactive monthly cash flow bar charts (Income vs. Expense) and category donut charts with touch inspection.
- **🔍 Fast Search & Filter** - Filter transactions instantly by expense, income, or transfer, and search across titles, categories, notes, and amounts.
- **🎨 Custom Categories & Avatars** - Create personalized income and expense categories with vibrant colors and custom icons, paired with playful emoji avatars.
- **🌍 Global Multi-Currency** - Seamlessly switch between USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), INR (₹), JPY (¥), CAD, AUD, and more with localized number formatting.
- **🔒 100% Offline & Private** - Your financial data never leaves your phone. Full local storage with one-tap JSON backup and restore to your clipboard.

---

### ⚡ WHY EXPENSY?

- **Fast & Distraction-Free** - Clean, modern design system engineered for effortless daily logging without bloated menus.
- **Total Financial Privacy** - Zero account sign-ups, zero internet permissions required for data storage, and zero data collection.
- **Complete Data Ownership** - Instant full-data JSON export and import so your financial records are always portable and safe.

Track every penny. Master your money. Take control of your financial journey with Expensy!
